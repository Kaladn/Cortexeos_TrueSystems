### Child C1 — symbol-law correction

Ordinary words and raw strings remain unchanged and unsymbolized. Symbolization is reserved for complex objects, containers, embedded objects, and otherwise unwieldy permanent identities.

### Child C2 — object custody

A photograph, chart, graph, movie, embedded media item, or complete conversation input receives a parent identity. Derived OCR, descriptions, relations, temporal logs, and structural views remain children of that parent.

### Child C3 — bracketing law

The same parent marker opens and closes the derived representation. Everything between the markers is governed by that parent. The authoritative source remains outside the projection and is never replaced by it.

### Child C4 — view, do not rewrite

Block/line/object and 6-1-6 are projections applied while looking at the original source. They are not normalized replacement documents. Exact source bytes remain authoritative.

### Child C5 — embedded-object law

An object embedded in a document inherits the document's custody path while retaining its own child identity and derived relationship field. A standalone object receives normal TrueVision intake and its own dataset-local custody path.

### Child C6 — hierarchy

Dataset → document/conversation → input/output turn → embedded object or inserted block → derived observations/relations → exact source coordinates.
